Subprocessors and Service Providers

The App runtime uses Atlassian-hosted services only. Separate providers used for the public website and support are identified below so customers can distinguish App data from vendor-site data.

App runtime and customer support

ProviderPurposeData involvedLocation
Atlassian Pty Ltd and relevant Atlassian group entitiesJira Cloud, Jira Service Management, Forge compute/storage, async services, platform logs/metrics, Marketplace licensing, optional Forge LLM and Mederak Apps Service Desk.Customer Jira/JSM data permitted to the App, App configuration and records, trusted site/user context, optional AI input/output, support tickets.Customer-selected or Atlassian-managed regions according to the service, site configuration and Atlassian documentation.

The App has no vendor-hosted remote, external App database, third-party product analytics or direct external AI integration. Forge LLM is supplied through Atlassian; Atlassian may use its own subprocessors for the platform as described in Atlassian's current disclosures.
