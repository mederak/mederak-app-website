Support Service Level Agreement

This page states the support availability and target first response for Customer Portal Toolkit for JSM.

Support intake

Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Target first response

Within 24 hours after receipt.

Support channel

Mederak Apps Service Desk

Languages

English and Polish.

What the target means

The initial response may acknowledge the request, ask triage questions, provide a workaround or confirm that investigation has started. The target is not a guaranteed resolution time, uptime commitment, service credit or guarantee that an Atlassian platform incident can be resolved by Mederak Apps.

Coverage

The target applies to requests for the current supported Marketplace or authorized early-access version, provided through the stated Service Desk. Response and resolution may depend on safe diagnostics, customer cooperation, Jira/JSM configuration, Atlassian services, Marketplace processes and defect complexity.

Security reports

Potential security or privacy incidents are prioritized. Put Security report in the summary and follow Security Incident Response. Do not send credentials, customer content or unsafe exploit material in the initial ticket.

Detailed policy

Severity, scope, exclusions, safe ticket content and maintenance handling are described in the Support Policy. The general Mederak Apps support commitment is also published at Mederak Apps Support SLA.
